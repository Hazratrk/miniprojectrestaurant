About Ferrero Restaurant

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Ferrero Restaurant began as a small family-owned establishment with a passion for authentic Italian cuisine combined with local Azerbaijani flavors.

Over the years, we've grown into one of Baku's most beloved dining destinations, known for our commitment to quality ingredients and exceptional service.

Our name "Ferrero" pays homage to the rich culinary traditions of Italy while embracing our local heritage.
